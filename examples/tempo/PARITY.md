# Tempo's parity with a desktop streaming app

What a daily user of a desktop music-streaming app expects, and where Tempo stands. Layout and behaviour only: every
name, picture, song and line of text is Tempo's own, and the icons are Lucide's. ✅ done, ◐ partly, ✗ not yet.
